# Sample findings

This report was generated from **2,500 synthetic customers** and **6,146 synthetic orders** with a fixed random seed. The figures below describe only this practice dataset.

- Completed orders: **5,532**
- Completed-order revenue: **$702,073.23**
- Customers with a completed order: **2,439**
- Repeat customer rate: **72.32%** (1,764 customers with at least two completed orders)
- Highest-revenue acquisition channel in this sample: **Search** ($238,591.35)
- December revenue month-over-month change: **-14.36%**

## How to read this

These results are generated from a simple simulation with a fixed channel-specific repeat-purchase probability. They demonstrate metric definitions and SQL workflow; they do not imply that an acquisition channel causes retention or that these rates apply to a real store. Cohort month 0 is the signup month. Cancelled and refunded orders are excluded from completed-order revenue.
